## Notes on the board

> Sirpeltric Project — Background references

> Environment / background artist board · open a folder on the left for each background

> One continuous camera journey through anime history — every background carries the scene into the next era,
> from 1945 black & white to full colour, then back into a grey warp at the end.

> 1 · 1945 · Black & white
> Wartime feature look
> Sc 5, 13 · grey warp 24

> 4 · 1970s
> Warm cel, poster paint
> Sc 11–12

> 7 · Full colour
> Painterly, modern
> Sc 17–21

> BG 1 · World reveal — City view
> Scenes 3–6 · 1960s → 1945 B&W → colour
> 
> Big establishing reveal: the camera rises and pulls back, the city unfolds layer by layer around one hero landmark.
> • 360° turn — extra info coming from the team.

> BG 2 · Earth zoom out + Globe 360°
> Scenes 7–9 · base style → 1950s
> 
> Continuous zoom street → city → continent → globe; the globe as a gouache storybook painting on a 360° turntable.
> • Clouds on their own layer.

> BG 3 · Underwater
> Scene 10 · 1960s graphic sea
> 
> Falling into the sea: coral reef and fish, light shafts and caustics, deeper = bluer.
> • Poki spins and a crack opens to the next era.

> BG 4 · Mirrored background
> Scenes 11–18 · 1970s → 1980s → 1990s
> 
> Mirrored platform with the horizon as the mirror line; later a 4-box mirrored BG with a colour per side.
> • Realistic reflection reference only.

> BG 5 · Hobby background
> Scenes 19–20 · full colour, painterly
> 
> Cosy hobby space for the character.
> • Recycle a room BG from earlier animations and re-dress it with new props.

> BG 6 · Forward pathway — Scene 22
> Scenes 21–24 · colour → grey warp
> 
> One-point path toward the camera, loopable; ends in the last warp hole — greyscale, then black.
> • Keep the path centre clear for the characters.

> HOW WE WORK
> • One background per week — every deadline is a Friday (see Planner)
> • Post progress updates in the SirPeltric Studio Discord group during the week
> • Questions → the team on Discord (Sir Peltric, Keinn)
> • Each folder holds references for that background only; era references live in 00 · Anime era timeline
> • Press Help (top bar) for how the board works

> BEFORE SENDING A BACKGROUND
> ☐ Kawaii & simple: rounded shapes, cheerful colour, not busy behind the characters
> ☐ Camera move matches the storyboard
> ☐ Era colour treatment applied
> ☐ Layers split: foreground / midground / background / sky
> ☐ Resolution & file format confirmed with the team
> ☐ Preview posted on Discord before the final file

> ART DIRECTION — KAWAII & SIMPLE
> The feel of Kirby and Chiikawa — not realism, not overly detailed.
> • Push the roundness: rounded shapes, softer forms, simple playful details
> • Colourful and lively, with an overall happy, cozy tone
> • Characters stay the main focus — backgrounds have personality but never feel busy
> 
> HOW TO USE THE REFERENCES
> • Real photos stay: they show how the place really looks — study them, then simplify into round, cute shapes
> • Where a matching anime / cartoon reference exists, it sits next to the real one to show the simplified look
> 
> Keep the era colour steps from the storyboard, drawn in this simpler, rounder style.

> Simple style references — round, cheerful, few details
> (Sir Peltric also shared a Chiikawa field and Kirby pixel scenes in Discord — drop them here.)

> Follows the storyboard order. Each era change passes through the red wave line. References are for internal / client use only.

> 1945 LOOK · scenes 5, 13, 24
> • Pure black & white — values only, no hue
> • Soft vignette, film grain, light flicker / gate weave
> • Simple flat BGs, rounded rubber-hose motion
> • Camera: static or slow truck out

> 1950s LOOK · scene 9
> • First colour features: soft gouache, pastel limited palette
> • Storybook / scroll-painting BGs, gentle light
> • Round full animation (Disney influence)
> • Era change enters on the red wave line

> 1960s LOOK · scenes 4 (reveal) & 10 (sea)
> • Limited animation: held poses, flat colour, bold outline
> • Graphic, design-led BGs (modernist shapes)
> • Sea: patterned waves, flat blue bands, circle bubbles
> • Early TV often B&W; features bright & flat

> 1970s LOOK · scenes 11–12
> • Warm cel colour, poster-paint BGs, visible brushwork
> • Realistic hand-painted landscapes & skies
> • Earthy browns, olive greens, faded film colour
> • Thicker lines, grain

> 1980s LOOK · scene 15
> • Saturated colour, airbrushed skies & glows
> • Very detailed painted BGs, dramatic backlight
> • Neon pink / purple nights, strong cast shadows
> • 2–3 tone cel shading on characters

> 1990s LOOK · scene 16
> • Late cel → early digital paint, cooler muted palette
> • Teal / cyan cast, soft haze, layered depth
> • Clean line, crisp shadow shapes, subtle grain
> • Wide shots with atmospheric BGs

> FULL COLOUR · scenes 17–21
> • Rich painterly BGs, bright daylight, strong colour story
> • 4-box: same BG mirrored, colour changes per quadrant
> • Hobby places: lived-in detail, warm light
> • Slow truck out → BG fades → grey warp (24)
