# BACKGROUND 3 · UNDERWATER (storyboard scene 10)
**Era (brief):** Sc 10 = **1960s** graphic sea (patterned waves, flat blue bands, clean circle bubbles, bold outline), and at the end a **crack** opens to the next era (**1970s**). The era dropdown offers 1960s · 1970s only.
**Deadline (plan):** week 3 · Fri 23 Oct 2026.

## Shots
1. **Splash in**: the camera drops through the bright surface into the water; a bubble burst.
2. **Descent**: a slow vertical drop past the light shafts to the reef (turquoise near the top → deep blue below; reds fade first). → **Panorama: one tall vertical strip** of the whole descent (surface to sea floor), stitched from a near-orthographic view so layers never smear.
3. **Reef hold**: a calm framed reef with an empty, low-contrast centre where Poki will be animated (no model). → Panorama: a slow lateral track along the reef, one long flat strip (near-orthographic).
- Extra button: **Caustic loop (12 frames ZIP)**, the floor element over one caustic period (1 s), so the loop can be cycled in compositing.
4. **Crack → 1970s**: the camera turns slowly (matching Poki's spin); the crack shader splits the frame and the 1970s look shows through the gap. Storyboard reference: Doctor Strange 2 multiverse, youtu.be/x0oQHPOSHEg?t=15 (00:15–00:19).

## The sea (kawaii + 1960s graphic)
- **Plate coral**: stacked rounded discs in teal-blue (the main shape of Doraemon *Mermaid King* and Kirby Everbay). **Branch coral** in magenta / purple (soft capsules), **brain coral** (bumpy spheres, pink / orange), **tube coral** clusters, **fan coral** (soft discs), round lavender **rocks**, swaying **kelp** (capsule chains), **sea grass** tufts, a sand floor with soft dunes.
- **Fish shoals** (simple rounded fish, yellow / orange / blue-stripe) in layered schools; friendly background creatures: a sea turtle, a seahorse, a crab (Doraemon *Mermaid King*). These are scenery, not the characters.
- **Bubbles**: clean outlined circles rising; marine snow specks.
- **Light shafts** from the surface (own element, transparent), **caustics** (an animated Voronoi network) on the floor and coral tops, with an 8–12 frame loop.
- In the distance, a soft pastel shell-tower coral town silhouette in the haze (One Piece Ryugu / Fish-Man Island is the main style target, simplified).

## Depth planes (FG · MG · BG · far)
- FG (< 6, darkest): camera-locked frame under water — kelp + branch coral on the left edge, plate rocks, fan coral and sea grass bottom-right. Off above the water (splash).
- MG (< 40): the reef and Poki's calm sand patch.
- BG (< 110): far ledges, shell towers; far: the fog wall, lightest.
