## Two sets in one file
**A · The hobby room** (recyclable: the shell and perspective stay fixed, the props change):
- Chiikawa-simple shell: cream walls with ink dashes, a warm wood floor, wooden ceiling beams (Kirby's room / We Bare Bears cave), one window (square with a cross frame, or round as an option) with curtains (blue with star dots as in Kirby's room).
- Furniture, all soft and rounded: a low round pink table (Chiikawa), a bed with a puffy blue star blanket (Kirby), a desk under the window with a frilly pink lamp (Whisper of the Heart), a bookshelf with rounded books (Kiki), a striped pastel rug (Kiki), a purple floor cushion (Kirby), a fireplace corner, potted plants.
- **Hobby prop sets** (GUI; the hobby is not confirmed yet): Drawing (easel, pencil cups, paper stacks), Music (guitars on a stand, a small keyboard, headphones), Games (a rounded console, a small round TV, cushions), Plants (round pots, watering can), Crafts / sewing (sewing table, yarn balls, spools).
- Warm window light and a lamp, a cozy afternoon. A locked 2-point perspective guide (screen only).
- **Panorama:** a slow pan across the room (about 110°), one wide strip.

**B · The run-cycle strip** (Sc 19–20): a horizontally tiling landscape behind the (not modelled) running characters. Segments: a hobby place (a small workshop / studio front), a lake with lavender mountains and pastel snow caps (Kirby pixel scene), and a golden field with round trees. Each segment has its own colour story. Layers at different depths give natural parallax; the strip repeats seamlessly.
- **Panorama:** the strip is a side view (near-orthographic camera, 5,000 units away), so the export is one flat strip of exactly one loop (180 units ≈ 9,700 px at 2160 px tall) that tiles seamlessly; each element (ground, lake, mountains, trees, field, fences, flowers, clouds, the hobby place) is its own PNG for parallax.
