# BACKGROUND 6 · FORWARD PATHWAY (storyboard scenes 21–24, scene 22 is the main one)
**Era (brief):** Sc 21–22 **full colour** (the background fades during the truck-out) · Sc 23–24 the jump into the last warp hole: **greyscale, then black** (the bookend back to 1945 values). The era dropdown offers full colour · grey warp only.
**Deadline (plan):** week 6 · Fri 13 Nov 2026.

## Shots
1. **Sc 21 · slow truck out**: the camera pulls back while the background washes out (lighter, less saturated).
2. **Sc 22 · dolly zoom on the path**: the characters (not modelled) walk toward the camera; the camera dollies while the FOV changes (fov = 2·atan(h / 2d)), so a subject point keeps its size while the background stretches. The path **loops seamlessly** (repeating modules wrap around the camera).
3. **Sc 23–24 · warp hole**: a push into a Doraemon "Time Tunnel" style warp (swirling purple / teal stripes, floating ring clocks), then grey, then black.

## The path (kawaii)
- A soft cream / sand path with rounded raised edges, straight to the vanishing point on the eye line; the path centre stays clear.
- **Repeating module** (length 12): a pair of leafy round trees (Kirby Green Greens / Waddle Dee Town, built from many leaf clumps on a tapered trunk with branches; ≈ 30 % taller ones), lumpy bushes, one big FG bush cluster right at the path edge, a little lamp post, flowers, grass clumps, a rock or a fallen log now and then, a wooden fence (WBB road). Optional tree-tunnel arches (Kawaguchiko momiji tunnel, English tree tunnels) and a pine-tree style (WBB).
- At the vanishing point: a soft mountain with a pastel snow cap (Fuji at the end of the momiji tunnel), foothills either side (2 massifs each, with shoulders) and puffy outlined clouds.
- The 18 modules are grouped three at a time; a group wraps around the camera only once it is fully behind it, so the path never shows a gap.
- Nothing bare: the grass sides carry tufts, dashes, flowers and stones.

## Exports
- In the default *long panorama* format the colour / lineart / base buttons export the side strip below. Switch *PNG format* to *Single 4K frame* to export forward frames of the push / dolly zoom (and the per-element ZIP as multiplane layers).
- **Panorama:** a long strip of the **path module rows seen from the side** (near-orthographic side camera tracking along the path, one full loop of 18 modules), so the tree, fence and flower rows can be painted once and reused for the loop. The warp tunnel exports as 4K frames (a push-in has no sideways travel).

## Depth planes (FG · MG · BG · far)
- FG (< 7, darkest): big bushes right at the path edge in every module, so they sweep past the lens during the truck-out and the dolly zoom (world space, real parallax).
- MG (< 40): the path, lamps, fences, near trees.
- BG (< 200): the tree tunnel; far: Fuji and clouds, lightest. The warp hole has no planes.
