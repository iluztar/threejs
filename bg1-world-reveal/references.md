## Notes on the board

> Big establishing reveal of the city: camera rises / pulls back to show the whole world for the first time. Scale, atmosphere and a clear focal landmark.

> Colour palette (extracted from the references)

> ERA FOR THIS SCENE
> • Sc 3–4: 360° rotation reveals the city in 1960s style
> • Sc 5: everything turns 1945 black & white (truck out)
> • Sc 6: pan track — colour slowly bleeds back in
> Full era notes → folder 00 · Anime era timeline

> DIRECTION
> • Reveal = camera crane-up / pull-back, city unfolds layer by layer
> • Strong atmospheric perspective: warm near, hazy cool far
> • One hero landmark as focal point (rule of thirds)
> • Golden-hour or blue-hour light for mood

> CHECKLIST
> ☐ Thumbnail 3 compositions
> ☐ Lock camera move with animator
> ☐ Split layers: FG / MG / BG / sky for parallax
> ☐ Colour key approved
> ☐ Final paint + light pass

> WBB CITY LOOK
> • San Francisco read through soft storybook shapes
> • Loose, slightly off-kilter line — perspective felt, not ruled
> • Muted pastels + warm light; buildings as simple blocks with few, clear details

> STORYBOARD REFERENCE
> Scene 6 — pan track: screen slowly begins to have colour
> Watch 00:21 – 00:24 — press ▶ on the card, it plays only this part.
> 
> Link from the storyboard: https://youtu.be/w9BYk8unGoQ?t=21

> 360° PAN — HOW TO DRAW IT
> • Draw only the BLUE frames — 3 panels across the turn
> • Centre panel, straight in front of the camera = a flat image
> • The two diagonal blue panels = where the perspective shifts
> • RED panels at the far left / right = smear (motion blur), no detailed drawing
> • Snow made a quick 3D mockup to show how the movement should feel
> 
> From Snow in the team's Discord, Fri 2 Oct — for Sir Peltric & Keinn to see too.

> Your mood board is very clear and concise. I can easily tell this is going to take some time to fully accomplish, but I really like the direction.
> 
> For the backgrounds, let’s keep them kawaii and simple, similar to the feel of Kirby and Chiikawa. I don’t want us leaning too heavily into realism or making the environments overly detailed.
> 
> Let’s push the roundness more—rounded shapes, softer forms, and simple, playful details. I want the environments to feel colorful and lively, but still have an overall happy and cozy tone.
> 
> The characters should remain the main focus, so the backgrounds can have personality without feeling too busy. Think cute, colorful, rounded, and cheerful rather than realistic or highly detailed.
> 
> I think that will fit the overall direction of the project really well.
>
