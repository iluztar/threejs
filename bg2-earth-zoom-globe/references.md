## Notes on the board

> Zoom out from the city to space, ending on the whole Earth. Includes 360° globe turntable references for rotation, clouds and day/night terminator.

> Colour palette (extracted from the references)

> ERA FOR THIS SCENE
> • Sc 7–8: jump over the edge & fall (base style)
> • Sc 9: red wave line passes → whole scene in 1950s style
> • Globe as a gouache storybook painting, pastel sky
> Full era notes → folder 00 · Anime era timeline

> DIRECTION
> • Continuous zoom: street → city → coastline → continent → globe
> • Log-scale camera speed (slow near, fast mid, slow at the end)
> • Globe: 360° turntable, 24–30 s per rotation, soft atmosphere rim
> • Clouds on a separate layer, rotate slightly faster than land

> GLOBE 360° CHECKLIST
> ☐ Texture: Blue Marble NG (equirectangular)
> ☐ Clouds layer + night lights (Black Marble)
> ☐ Terminator (day/night edge) soft falloff
> ☐ Rim light / atmosphere halo
> ☐ Loopable 360° turn for comp

> WBB WIDE SHOTS
> • Big skies with soft cloud shapes, simple layered land
> • Postcard framing for the zoom-out beats
> • Keep the palette muted so the globe reveal still pops
