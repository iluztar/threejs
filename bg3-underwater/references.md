## Notes on the board

> Underwater environment: light shafts from the surface, caustics on the sea floor, depth fog shifting to blue-green.

> Colour palette (extracted from the references)

> DIRECTION
> • Reds disappear first — deeper = cooler, bluer
> • God rays from above (Color Dodge, low opacity)
> • Caustic network on floor & large forms, animated loop
> • Floating particles / marine snow for depth

> CHECKLIST
> ☐ Depth value scale (surface → deep)
> ☐ Caustics loop (8–12 frames)
> ☐ Light-shaft layer separate
> ☐ Kelp / coral silhouettes for FG parallax

> ERA FOR THIS SCENE
> • Sc 10: falling into the sea; Poki spins, a crack opens to the next era
> • 1960s graphic sea: patterned waves, simple coral shapes
> • Flat blue bands, clean circle bubbles, bold outline
> Full era notes → folder 00 · Anime era timeline

> UNDERWATER LOOK — take from these
> • Coral reef in the foreground/midground, fish shoals in layers
> • Light shafts + caustics from the surface, bubbles rising
> • Colour: turquoise near the top → deep blue at the bottom
> • One Piece Fish-Man Island = the main style target (ep. 523–526 for the descent)
