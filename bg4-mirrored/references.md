## Notes on the board

> A mirrored world: perfect reflection / symmetry, horizon acting as a mirror line — calm, dreamlike, slightly surreal.

> Colour palette (extracted from the references)

> DIRECTION
> • Mirror line = horizon; reflection slightly darker & softer
> • Symmetry, but break it with small ripples / details
> • Consider 'folding' variant (Inception) for transitions

> CHECKLIST
